Hello, I’m Madison

I plan weddings for people who would rather remember the day than the timeline.

I started Hazel after planning my own wedding and realizing the part I loved most was the part everyone else dreaded.

I take on a small number of weddings each year and stay with each one from the first budget conversation to the last vendor payment. I build the timeline, hold the vendor relationships, and stand at the back of the room on the day making sure nothing needs you. You show up, get married, and spend the evening with your people.

Why hire a planner

Someone should be watching the clock. It shouldn’t be you.

A wedding is a few hundred small decisions and about forty people who all need to be in the right place at the right time. My job is to have made the decisions with you months ago, so that on the day there is nothing left to decide.

  • i.A timeline every vendor has seen. Built weeks out, shared with everyone, and protected on the day. Nobody asks you what happens next.
  • ii.One person to call. The florist calls me. The caterer texts me. Your mother finds me. You get to be a guest at your own wedding.
  • iii.Someone who has read every contract. Overtime clauses, rain plans, load-in windows. I catch the things that turn into surprises later.
  • iv.A calm person at the back of the room. If a thing goes wrong, and one small thing always does, you will hear about it after the honeymoon, if at all.

How it works

Simple, from the first hello to the last dance.

Say hello

Fill out the inquiry form with your date and what you’re picturing. I reply within two business days with availability and a short guide to the collections.

Meet

A relaxed video call or a coffee. I ask a lot of questions, you ask a lot of questions, and we sketch what working together could look like.

Reserve your date

A signed agreement and a retainer holds the day. From here you get a shared planning portal, timeline templates and a direct line to me.

Get married

I run the day. You live it. Afterward I return the rentals, settle the final invoices and send you the last of the details while you’re on your honeymoon.
