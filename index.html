Hello, we’re Hazel

A wedding studio run by two people who happen to be married.

We started Hazel because we kept watching couples hire a planner, a photographer and a videographer who had never met, then spend their wedding day translating between them.

So we built the thing we wished existed: a single team that designs the timeline around the light, coordinates the vendors, and is already standing in the right spot when the moment happens. Fewer strangers, fewer handoffs, and a day that moves at the pace of your life instead of a shot list.

The Hazel difference

Why one team changes everything.

Most weddings are run by three vendors who meet for the first time at 2 p.m. on the day. Ours are run by two people who planned the timeline together at your kitchen table.

  • i.Timelines built around light. Your planner and photographer are the same team, so portraits land in golden hour instead of fighting the schedule.
  • ii.No shot-list stress. Family formals, details and candids are already in the run-of-show. You never have to remember to ask.
  • iii.One point of contact. One contract, one inbox, one person to text at 11 p.m. the night before.
  • iv.Bundle pricing. Book two or three of our services together and the collection is priced accordingly.

How it works

Simple from the first hello to the final gallery.

Say hello

Fill out the inquiry form with your date and what you’re dreaming about. We reply within two business days with availability and a short guide to our collections.

Meet us

A relaxed video call or coffee. We ask a lot of questions, you ask a lot of questions, and we sketch what your team could look like.

Reserve your date

A signed agreement and a 25% retainer holds the day. From here you get our planning portal, timeline templates and a direct line to us.

Live your wedding

We run the day, capture the day, and send sneak peeks within 48 hours. Full galleries arrive in six to eight weeks; films in ten to twelve.
