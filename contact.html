FAQ

Before you ask.

The questions we hear most. Planning- and photo-specific ones live on the planning page and the photo & film page.

How far in advance should we book?
Popular Utah dates (May through October Saturdays) tend to go 10 to 14 months out. Photo and film book first, planning second. That said, we’ve pulled off beautiful weddings in eight weeks; if your date is soon, ask anyway.
How do we hold our date?
A signed agreement and a 25% retainer. Dates aren’t held on a verbal yes, only because we’ve had to disappoint couples before and hated it.
Can we book just one service?
Of course. Plenty of couples hire us only for photography, or only for coordination. The bundles exist because working as one team makes the day better, not because we require it.
Do you work with couples who already have a photographer or planner?
Yes, and gladly. We coordinate with outside vendors all the time. If you already have a planner, we’ll get on their timeline. If you already have a photographer, our coordinator will make their life easy.
Do you photograph and film LGBTQ+ weddings, interfaith weddings, courthouse weddings?
Yes. Every couple, every kind of ceremony, every size of day. If two people are promising each other something, we want to be there.
What happens if one of you is sick on our wedding day?
We keep a small bench of trusted planners, photographers and cinematographers whose work we’d put our name on. In the rare case one of us can’t be there, a vetted professional steps in at no extra cost and we handle every edit and deliverable ourselves.
Is a deposit refundable?
Retainers are non-refundable because they take your date off the market. If you need to move your date, we transfer everything to the new one whenever we’re available, at no charge.
Do you offer payment plans?
Every collection can be split into monthly payments between booking and 30 days before the wedding. No interest, no fees.
