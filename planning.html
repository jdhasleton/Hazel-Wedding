The planning arc

What the year looks like with us.

A typical full-service or partial timeline. Shorter engagements are welcome; we simply compress the arc.

Foundation

12+ months. Budget, guest count, priorities, venue. We lock the two or three decisions everything else depends on.

Team

8–12 months. Photography and film (hi), catering, florals, music. The vendors who book out first get booked first.

Design

5–8 months. Palette, tabletop, paper, lighting. Mock-ups where they matter. Rentals ordered.

Details

2–5 months. Invitations out, RSVPs in, timeline drafted, hair and makeup trials, tastings, transportation.

Handoff

6 weeks to the day. We take over every vendor conversation. You stop answering emails and start looking forward to it.

Planning FAQ

Questions couples ask us.

More FAQs
What’s the difference between a planner and a coordinator?
A planner helps you make the decisions: budget, vendors, design, logistics, over months. A coordinator executes decisions you’ve already made, starting roughly six weeks out and running through the wedding day. Our Partial and Full Service collections include both; Month-of Coordination is just the second half.
Do we need a planner if our venue has a coordinator?
Venue coordinators are wonderful and they work for the venue. They make sure the building runs; they don’t manage your florist, cue your processional, fix your bustle or keep your timeline. It is a different job, and on the day you want someone whose only client is you.
Can we start with The Blueprint and upgrade later?
Yes, and couples do this often. Your $950 is credited in full toward Partial or Full Service if you upgrade at least four months before the wedding, subject to availability.
How many weddings do you take?
We cap full-service and partial planning at a small number each year and never book two weddings on the same weekend. When you hire us, you get us, not a rotating team.
Do you plan weddings outside Utah?
Happily. We plan destination weddings anywhere; travel is quoted separately and we’re transparent about it up front. Ask about our destination collection, which bundles planning with photo and film so you’re only flying in one team.
What does the payment schedule look like?
A 25% retainer reserves your date. The balance is split into equal payments, with the final one due 30 days before the wedding. Add-ons booked later are invoiced as they’re added.
