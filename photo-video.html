How we shoot

Documentary first. Portraits that don’t feel posed.

We spend most of the day quietly documenting, and carve out two or three short portrait windows in the best light. You’ll get direction when you want it and space when you don’t.

  • i.Color you’ll still love in 20 years. No heavy presets, no orange skin, no trend that dates the gallery.
  • ii.Two of us, most of the time. Our Signature and Heirloom collections include a second photographer, so both of you are covered getting ready.
  • iii.Sneak peeks in 48 hours. A set of finished favorites while the day is still fresh, then the full gallery in six to eight weeks.
  • iv.Films cut from your audio. Vows and toasts drive the edit; music supports it. It should sound like your day.

Photo & film FAQ

Good questions.

More FAQs
How many hours do we actually need?
For most weddings with a first look, 8 hours covers the last hour of getting ready through the open dancing. Without a first look, or with a long gap between ceremony and reception, 10 hours keeps the day relaxed. Six hours works beautifully for intimate weddings and elopements. We’ll map it to your timeline before you book.
When will we see our photos and film?
Sneak peeks arrive within 48 hours. Full photo galleries are delivered in 6 to 8 weeks (4 weeks with Heirloom), and films in 10 to 12 weeks. Your social teaser lands within the first two weeks.
Do we get the raw files?
Photography: we deliver every image we’d put our name on, fully edited, and we don’t release unedited RAW files. Film: a raw footage archive is included in Legacy and available as an add-on to any other collection.
Can we choose the music for our film?
You tell us the feeling and any songs that matter to you; we source licensed tracks that fit so your film can live online without takedowns. If you have a specific commercial song, we can license it for you at cost.
Do you travel?
Yes, anywhere. Travel within 60 miles of Salt Lake City is included. Beyond that we quote mileage and, when needed, lodging at cost. Destination weddings are some of our favorite work.
What if it rains, or the venue bans drones?
We plan a covered portrait option with you in advance and we bring lighting for dark rooms. If drone flight isn’t possible for weather or airspace reasons, we credit the drone fee or swap it for an add-on of equal value.
