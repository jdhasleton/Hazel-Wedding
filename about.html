About

Two people, one studio, and a soft spot for good light.

We planned our own wedding, photographed each other’s families for years, and finally admitted the thing we most wanted to do was this. Hazel is the studio we wished we could have hired.

Our story

Why “Hazel”?

Hazel is the color of an eye that can’t decide between green and brown. It’s warm, a little unexpected, and it changes with the light. That felt right for what we make.

We met the way a lot of people do now, and married the way fewer people do: in California, with a planner we adored, a photographer we found on a hunch, and a videographer who introduced himself to both of them at two o’clock on the wedding day. Everyone was talented. Nobody had ever worked together. We spent an alarming amount of our own wedding translating.

Afterward, back home in Utah, we kept talking about it. One of us had been organizing events and people for years and secretly loved a spreadsheet. The other had been carrying a camera everywhere since high school and had quietly started filming friends’ weddings. It took a while to say it out loud: we should do this together, for other couples, as one team.

So that’s Hazel. One of us plans and runs the day. The other photographs and films it. We build the timeline together, around the light and around you, and we show up on the morning already knowing where the good moments are going to happen.

We take a small number of weddings each year on purpose. We’d rather know your grandmother’s name than have a bigger calendar.

What we believe

A few things we’ll never compromise on.

Prices on the page

You shouldn’t have to book a call to find out whether you can afford us. Everything we offer is listed with its price, and it’s the same price for everyone.

Your day, not our portfolio

We’ll suggest, we’ll steer, we’ll tell you when something won’t photograph well. But we never move your wedding around to get a shot.

Small on purpose

We cap our calendar so we can give every couple the same care. When you hire Hazel you get the two of us, not a team you meet on the day.
