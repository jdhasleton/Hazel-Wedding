About

Hi, I’m Madison. I plan weddings people actually get to enjoy.

Hazel is a small wedding planning studio based in Utah. It’s me, a very good day-of team, and a soft spot for a well-run timeline.

My story

Why “Hazel”?

Hazel is the color of an eye that can’t decide between green and brown. It’s warm, a little unexpected, and it changes with the light. That felt right for the kind of weddings I want to make.

I planned my own wedding in California with a spreadsheet, a very patient fiancé and no planner. I loved it. Not the stress, exactly, but the part where forty small decisions turned into one good day, and the part where everyone I love was in the same room and nobody had to think about the schedule.

Afterward, friends started asking for help with theirs. Then friends of friends. At some point it stopped being a favor and became the thing I most wanted to do. Hazel Wedding Co. is the result: a small planning studio in Utah, where I grew up and where we live now.

I take a limited number of weddings each year, on purpose. I would rather know your grandmother’s name and your caterer’s cell number than have a bigger calendar. On the day, I’m the person at the back of the room with the timeline, the emergency kit and the quiet word with the DJ, so that the only thing you have to do is get married.

What I believe

A few things I won’t compromise on.

Honest about money

You shouldn’t have to book a call to find out whether you can afford me. Every collection is listed with what it includes and what it costs, and it’s the same price for everyone.

Your day, not my portfolio

I’ll suggest, I’ll steer, I’ll tell you when something won’t work in the room. But I never move your wedding around to make it look better online.

Small on purpose

I cap my calendar so I can give every couple the same care. When you hire Hazel, you get me from the first call to the last dance, not a team you meet on the day.
